Patient accounts

Patient accounts are created by the dentist. Patients cannot register themselves.

If you are a patient, ask your dentist to create your account. They will give you a private link. Opening that link logs you in automatically and asks you to choose your own password.

After that, you can log in normally with your username and password. You can also update your name, username, and email at any time from the Account page.
