Dentist accounts

Dentist accounts are created by the clinic administrator. Dentists cannot register themselves.

If you are a dentist, ask the administrator to create your account. They will give you a username and a temporary password. The first time you log in, you will be asked to choose a new password before you can see your schedule.

After that, you can update your name, username, and email at any time from the Account page, and change your password there too.
